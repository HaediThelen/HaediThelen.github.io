I gave a podium presentation on weighting methods for internal derivation of disease risk scores to study treatment effect heterogeneity in real-world data at the 2026 International Society for Pharmacoepidemiology Annual Meeting in Milan.
The presentation sparked a lot of great questions and conversations about how we can make these methods work better in real-world settings. 
I left the meeting especially excited to keep developing this work and to see it through to publication.
